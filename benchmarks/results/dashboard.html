Register collapse — the corpus's central finding

Every generated session is tagged by how explicitly it states its facts. All four pattern-matching graph methods (TokenMizer included) land in a narrow ~19–25% band once marker words like "Completed:" or "Decided:" disappear — a >45-point drop from the explicit-text score. This is a shared ceiling of pattern-based extraction, not a TokenMizer-specific gap. (unspecified = the 6 real sessions carried over from the product repo's eval corpus, which predate the register tag.)

TokenMizer vs. every other method — paired difference

Mean of (TokenMizer macro F1 − other method macro F1), paired per session, with a 95% bootstrap CI. Bars crossing zero are statistically indistinguishable from a tie at this sample size and threshold.

Read it straight: TokenMizer beats GraphRAG-style (+12.5pt), MemGPT-style (+21.6pt) and every naive baseline (+36 to +40pt) well outside noise. Against Graphiti-style and Mem0-style the CI straddles zero (P(Δ≤0) ≈ 91–92%) — a tie, not a win, despite TokenMizer's smaller resume-token footprint (99 vs. 118–120 tokens).
